03 / Practical demonstration

Email campaign QA checklist

A sample pre-send review framework showing how I would approach a campaign before it is scheduled. This is a self-created demonstration, not a record of campaigns sent for an employer.

✓Content supplied
Confirm approved copy, subject line and preview text are present.
✓Links checked
Open key links and confirm they lead to the intended destination.
✓Formatting reviewed
Check spacing, headings, images and mobile readability.
✓Audience verified
Confirm the selected segment matches the campaign instructions.
✓Send settings checked
Review sender details, schedule, time zone and send configuration.
✓Tracking reviewed
Check tracking parameters and campaign naming where provided.
✓Final approval
Resolve discrepancies and confirm readiness before scheduling.
✓Tracker updated
Record campaign status, schedule and any open dependencies.
My working principle: If a link, audience, asset, instruction or send time looks unusual, I would pause and flag it rather than guess.
